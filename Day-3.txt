Day-2 of Striver's DSA sheet

Topic was time and space complexity.

Time complexity: 
Rate at which time taken for program to execute increases with respect to the size of input.
Things to remember:
- Calculate Worst case scenario (BIG O)
- Avoid constants
- Avoid all lower values

Big O - Worst case 
Thetha - Average case
Omega - Best case

Space complexity:
Space to solve the problem and space to store the problem 
Calculated as Big O notations
